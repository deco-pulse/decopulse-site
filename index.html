DecoPulse help

DecoPulse is an app for discovering home décor from UK shops. Swipe right to save an item, left to pass.

Contact

For help with the app, email support@deco-pulse.com.

For anything about your information or privacy, email privacy@deco-pulse.com.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Everything works without one. An account backs up your saved items and shows them on your other devices.
  • Where did my saved items go after signing out? Saved items belong to the account. They come back when you sign in again.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Delete Account. This removes the account and everything stored with it.
  • How do I delete my swipe history but keep my account? In the app: Profile → Privacy → Delete My Usage Data.
  • A shop’s link doesn’t open. The item may have been removed by the shop. The catalogue is refreshed every week.
